Our story

Antipode began because two of us kept having the same argument in front of the same wardrobe. Every summer we bought for a northern-hemisphere winter that never arrives, and every autumn we were caught out by the one afternoon the wind swings around and the temperature falls eight degrees before the train home.

So the buy is organised around the weather we actually get. Heavy things that breathe. Shells that stop a southerly without turning into a sauna at twenty-two degrees indoors. Leather that survives a Brisbane February. Wool for the six weeks of the year Melbourne genuinely needs it.

We are named for being on the opposite side of the world from where most of this industry's calendar is set. That is a constraint, not a complaint. It is the whole reason the store is worth running.

We would rather be the store that has one good raincoat than the store that has forty.

Eight of the eighteen labels here are ours — Ostro, Kester, Halden, Marlowe & Vale, Ninth Parallel, Verity Rowe, Clarke Street and Bellamy. We make those where the market was not offering the piece at a price we thought was fair. The rest we buy because they are better at it than we would be.

How we buy

Three tests, applied twice a year

Does it solve something

It has to do a job nothing else in the buy already does. Two near-identical white shirts is a failure of buying, not a choice for the customer.

Will it last three years

Construction, fabric weight and how it is finished on the inside. If we cannot picture it in three years, it does not come in.

Would we take it back

We offer 30-day returns and pay the postage, so anything that disappoints comes straight back to us. That concentrates the mind.

Reviewed twice a year

Pieces that do not earn their place come out of the buy. The catalogue is small on purpose, and it stays that way.

Click and collect

Free from Surry Hills (Sydney), Fitzroy (Melbourne) and Fortitude Valley (Brisbane). Orders are on the counter within two business hours and held for fourteen days. Bring the confirmation email and photo ID.

Insured delivery

Anything over A$3,000 ships insured with signature on delivery at no extra cost, and cannot be left in a safe place or with a neighbour.

Tracking

Every order is tracked. You get the consignment number by email when it leaves us, and Australia Post will text you on the delivery day if you gave us a mobile number.

Returns and refunds

30 days, postage paid

Change your mind within 30 days of delivery and send it back for a full refund. The piece needs to be unworn, unwashed and with its tags still attached. We pay the return postage — the label is in your confirmation email, or request one from the order in your account.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within five business days of the parcel reaching us. Delivery charges are refunded too if the whole order goes back.

Sale items

Sale pieces are returnable on exactly the same terms. Nothing here is final sale.

Exceptions

  • Pierced earrings, for hygiene reasons, unless they arrive faulty.
  • Anything altered or resized at your request.
  • Fine watches are returnable within 30 days but must be unworn, with the protective film intact and all papers included.

You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure, and to compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if they fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure. These rights are in addition to our 30-day policy and are not limited by it.

Responsibility

A small buy is the most useful thing we do on this front. Sixty-three pieces means very little deadstock, and what does not sell goes to markdown rather than landfill.

  • Every house-label supplier is audited annually, and we publish the factory country on every product page.
  • Packaging is uncoated cardboard and paper tape. No plastic satchels, no tissue, no branded filler.
  • Returned pieces are inspected and resold. Nothing returned in sellable condition is destroyed.
  • Our Sydney workshop services watches and repairs leather, because a repair is better than a replacement.

We are not going to tell you that buying clothes is good for the planet. The most responsible thing on this site is the advice to buy less and keep it longer, which is why the journal spends more words on care than on trends.

Privacy

This is a demonstration storefront. It has no server and no analytics. Nothing you type into it is transmitted anywhere.

Your bag, wishlist, orders, saved addresses and size preferences are written to this browser's local storage on this device only. They are not sent to us, not shared, and not readable by any other site. Clearing your browser data removes them, and you can clear them yourself from the preferences tab of your account.

A real Antipode would handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, would tell you what it collects and why, and would let you ask for a copy or a deletion. This one collects nothing, so there is nothing to ask for.
